# LUNE Data Model

## Overview

LUNE uses structured JSON data to exchange information between conversational agents and automation workflows.

The data model is designed to maintain consistency across the pipeline while allowing each agent to perform a specific responsibility.

The lead's email is used as the primary identifier throughout the workflow.

## Lead Identity

The lead is identified by:

- `email`

The email is used to locate the corresponding lead record in the Google Sheets data store and to prevent duplicate records during the qualification stage.

## Agent 01 — Qualification & Diagnosis

The first stage produces the structured information required to qualify the lead and support the diagnosis.

The payload contains the lead's qualification information and the diagnostic information generated during the interaction.

The consolidated implementation introduced the following mandatory diagnostic fields:

- `diagnostico_validado`
- `funcionalidades_prioritarias`
- `argumentos_personalizados`
- `validacao_final`

When the lead is not qualified, these fields are populated with `não se aplica`, maintaining a consistent data structure across records.

## Agent 02 — Proposal

The proposal stage consumes the structured information produced during qualification and diagnosis.

The data used by this stage includes:

- lead identification;
- business information;
- qualification information;
- diagnostic information;
- prioritized functionalities;
- personalized arguments;
- proposal-related information;
- visual proposal information.

The proposal stage uses the structured data to generate and update the visual proposal and make the resulting information available to the presentation stage.

## Agent 03 — Presentation & Closing

The presentation and closing stage consumes the information generated by the previous stages.

The data exchanged with the presentation workflow includes:

- lead email;
- lead identification;
- briefing;
- visual proposal link;
- proposal information.

The closing workflow receives the final interaction data, including:

- email;
- name;
- budget;
- observations;
- preferred contact method;
- closing intention.

## Decision Data

The final interaction determines the next commercial route.

When the lead indicates an intention to close, the workflow follows the closing route.

When the lead does not indicate an intention to close, the workflow follows the nurturing/follow-up route.

## Data Flow

```text
Lead
  ↓
Qualification & Diagnosis
  ↓
Structured lead + diagnostic data
  ↓
Proposal
  ↓
Proposal + visual asset
  ↓
Presentation & Closing
  ↓
Decision
  ├── Closing
  └── Nurturing / Follow-up
```

## Data Integrity

The workflows use structured JSON payloads and validation rules to maintain consistency between the conversational agents and automation platforms.

The qualification workflow also checks whether the lead already exists before creating a new record, using the email as the primary key. This prevents duplicate records and supports idempotent execution.

## External Data Services

The structured data is persisted and exchanged through the services integrated into the pipeline:

Google Sheets
Google Drive
Cloudinary
Gmail

These services support data persistence, document storage, visual asset management, and communication respectively.
